## The Problem

Three small pains. One big relief.

**1. The 6 p.m. question.** Every weeknight, the same blank stare at the fridge. *"What do we eat?"* — and nobody has an answer.

**2. The budget surprise.** You walk out of the supermarket twenty euros over what you planned. Again. The receipt never matches the promise.

**3. The half-empty pantry.** You buy parsley for one recipe, use it twice, throw the rest away. Repeat every week.

---

## The Solution

**Three meals you'll actually cook.** Pantry-aware. Budget-friendly. Stress-free.

- **Meal planning in under sixty seconds.** Not a thousand recipes. Three coherent options, tuned to your week.
- **Auto-generated grocery list.** Consolidated, sorted by aisle, sync-ready with your shared notes.
- **Pantry-aware.** Tell us what's in your cupboards. We start from there — not from a food blogger's dream.

## How It Works

Three steps. Sunday morning, coffee in hand.

1. **Set your preferences** — household size, weekly budget, food no-go's. Takes two minutes, once.
2. **Auto-generate your week** — seven dinners, one grocery list, zero daily quiz.
3. **Shop smart** — list in hand, budget on track, no last-minute panic.

## FAQ

Yes. Your pantry, your budget, your household — encrypted at rest, never sold, never shared. You can delete everything in two clicks from your profile.

**2. Is there a free trial?**
The beta is fully free. When Pro launches, you'll get 30 days free before any charge — and your launch price is locked for life.

**3. Does it work on mobile?**
Yes. iOS and Android, tablet and phone. The grocery list works offline at the supermarket.

**4. Which countries are supported?**
Beta launches in **France, Belgium, Switzerland, and Canada (Québec)**. UK and Spain next quarter.

**5. Can I switch from another meal-planning app?**
Yes. Most competitors lock you in with recipes you'd lose. PlatePilot starts fresh from your pantry — give us ten minutes and you're set.
